Smileys & Emotions FAQs

What is the most popular smiley emoji in the world? ▾

Face with Tears of Joy (😂) consistently ranks among the most widely used emojis globally, alongside Rolling on the Floor Laughing (🤣) and Skull (💀).

Why does Gen Z use 💀 instead of 😂? ▾

💀 represents 'I'm dead from laughter'—an ironic, dry internet slang evolution that replaced the earnest crying-laughing smiley in many young online communities.
