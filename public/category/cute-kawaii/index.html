Cute & Kawaii FAQs

What makes an emoji 'Kawaii'? ▾

Kawaii is the Japanese culture of cuteness, characterized by innocence, soft pastel colors, oversized eyes, and charming animal or dessert motifs.

Which cute emojis are most popular for texting someone you like? ▾

🥺 (Pleading / Puppy eyes), 🧸 (Teddy bear), 🎀 (Pink bow), 🥹 (Touched / Emotional), and 🌸 (Cherry blossom).
