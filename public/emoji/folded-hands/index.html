What does the 🙏 Folded Hands mean in a text?

Signals profound respect, polite requests, and spiritual well-wishes.

Nuanced Contextual Interpretations

General Meaning
Two palms placed firmly together, universally recognized across global cultures for prayer, reverence, and gratitude.
Romantic Messages

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 🙏 a high five or prayer? ▾

According to Unicode and Japanese gesture traditions, it represents folded hands in prayer, respectful bowing, or saying 'thank you' (itadaki-masu), though some use it casually as a high five.
