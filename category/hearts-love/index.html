Hearts & Love FAQs

What do the different heart emoji colors mean? ▾

Red represents passionate romantic love; pink conveys sweet tenderness; white symbolizes pure condolences and sincerity; black expresses gothic style or sorrow; and heart-on-fire represents intense passion.

Which heart should I send to a casual friend? ▾

Pink (🩷), yellow (💛), two hearts (💕), or sparkling heart (💖) are traditionally preferred for close friendship without accidental romantic implications.
