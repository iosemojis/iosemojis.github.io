How do emoji skin tones work in Unicode? ▾

The Unicode Standard uses the Fitzpatrick scale (types 1-6) as modifier codepoints (U+1F3FB through U+1F3FF) combined with base characters to render diverse human skin tones.

What is a ZWJ sequence in people emojis? ▾

A Zero Width Joiner (ZWJ, U+200D) invisible character joins multiple emojis together (like a person + laptop = technologist) into a single combined glyph.
